A budget your agent cannot exceed.

The vault's authority is a 2-of-2 SPL Token multisig: your agent holds one key, the guard holds the other. A payment needs both signatures, so the chain itself refuses anything the guard did not co-sign. Policy: daily limit, per-transaction limit, recipient allowlist, kill switch. Every decision is recorded in a Memo instruction inside the transaction that moves the money.
